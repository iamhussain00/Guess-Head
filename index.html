How to Play "GuessHead"

1. The Forehead Secret (Post-it Rule)

Every player is assigned a secret character or word. In real life, it’s stuck to your forehead. On this site, everyone else can see your word, but your own card stays hidden with a mystery badge!

2. Voice Call Connection

Click "Voice Call Room" at any time to open the built-in Jitsi audio room so everyone can talk without needing third-party software.

3. Ask Yes-or-No Questions

On your turn, you have 60 seconds to ask yes/no questions to the group: "Am I alive?", "Am I a fictional character?", "Am I an animal?"

4. Solve or Pass

If you deduce your word, click "I Know My Word". If your card feels too obscure, you have 1 free card re-roll per round!
